ABOUT CITY BOY MOVEMENT OGUN

National purpose.
Local connection.

We are the Ogun State chapter of City Boy Movement, bringing the national movement closer to the communities of the Gateway State.

Our chapter brings people together around civic participation, community development and service. From Abeokuta to Ijebu-Ode, Sagamu to Ota, we create a local connection to a shared national purpose.

We stand behind President Bola Ahmed Tinubu, GCFR, our Grand Patron, and the Renewed Hope Agenda. We support his re-election through informed engagement, respectful dialogue and participation in public life.

This is a movement built around people: the communities we belong to, the responsibilities we share and the contributions we can make together.

THE PURPOSE WE SHARE

Our Vision & Mission

01

Our Vision

An Ogun State where every young person is informed, engaged and empowered: driving the growth of our towns and cities, strengthening democracy at the grassroots, and championing the Renewed Hope Agenda.

02

Our Mission

To organise young people across all 20 local government areas of Ogun State into a strong, active network that promotes the President’s policies, builds civic awareness, encourages voter participation, and connects youths to opportunities in enterprise and digital skills.
